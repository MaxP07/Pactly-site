2. What Paclara is

Paclara is a creator collaboration management application designed to help content creators organise the commercial side of their work.

Depending on the version and features available to you, Paclara may allow you to:

  • record and manage brand collaborations;
  • track deliverables and publication dates;
  • record fees, invoices and payment deadlines;
  • record exclusivity, non-compete and competitor-brand restrictions;
  • separately track advertising, paid-media and boosting periods;
  • review potential conflicts against information you have entered;
  • organise documents and attachments;
  • receive reminders;
  • use pricing-support features such as Price a Deal;
  • use supported on-device contract review features;
  • synchronise eligible data through iCloud where you choose to enable iCloud Sync; and
  • export or back up certain information.

Paclara is a planning, organisation and pricing-support tool. It does not act as your agent, manager, lawyer, accountant or financial adviser.
