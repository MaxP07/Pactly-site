Paclara Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

This policy explains how Max Poplawski ("we", "us") handles information when you use the Paclara iOS app, participate in its TestFlight beta, use the Paclara website, or contact support.

1. Information stored in Paclara

Paclara stores your information locally on your device. This may include collaboration details, publication and delivery dates, financial information, payment status, notes, links, briefs, contracts, attachments, agency and contact information, and other information you choose to enter.

Paclara does not operate a Paclara account system or a developer-operated server that stores your collaboration data.

You may optionally enable iCloud Sync. If you do, Paclara uses Apple’s CloudKit service and your private iCloud account to keep eligible Paclara data in sync between your Apple devices. This data is stored and transferred using Apple-managed iCloud infrastructure rather than a Paclara-operated server. iCloud Sync is optional and can be turned on or off in Paclara’s settings.

You may also choose to export a CSV report or a Paclara backup containing information you have entered. Exporting is initiated by you and Paclara does not send exported files to us. You choose where exported files are saved or shared. Paclara backup files are encrypted by Paclara before export. Anyone who obtains the backup file together with the information needed to open it may be able to access its contents. Deleting information from the app does not delete copies you previously exported.

The app may also communicate with Apple through Apple-managed services such as CloudKit and StoreKit. These communications are with Apple, not a Paclara-operated network service. Device backups may also include app data depending on your device, iCloud, and backup settings.

2. How information is used

Information stored in Paclara is used to provide features such as collaboration planning, conflict checking, reminders, pricing support, payment tracking, saved preferences, and, where enabled, synchronisation between your devices.

Paclara does not use an AI service to analyse your content and does not use your content for advertising.

3. iCloud Sync

iCloud Sync is optional. If you enable it, Paclara synchronises eligible app data through Apple’s private CloudKit database associated with your iCloud account. Paclara does not receive your iCloud password and does not operate the CloudKit infrastructure.

Changes made on one device, including edits and deletions, may be synchronised to your other devices that have iCloud Sync enabled for Paclara. Availability and operation of iCloud services are also subject to your Apple account, device settings, network connection, and Apple’s services and policies.

If you disable iCloud Sync, Paclara continues to use its local copy of your data on that device. Disabling sync does not itself delete your local Paclara library.

4. Recently Deleted

When you delete a collaboration, Paclara may place it in Recently Deleted for up to 72 hours before it becomes eligible for permanent deletion. During this period, you can restore it.

If iCloud Sync is enabled, the Recently Deleted state and later permanent deletion may also synchronise between your devices and through your private iCloud database. Copies may still remain temporarily in Apple-managed backups or other backup systems according to their own retention rules.

5. TestFlight beta testing

If you install Paclara through TestFlight, Apple automatically collects crash logs and usage information and shares them with us. This can include device and operating-system information, app sessions, and crashes. Tester names and email addresses may also be available, depending on how you joined the beta. Feedback you submit, including comments and screenshots, is shared with us by Apple.

We use this information to manage the beta, investigate problems, and improve Paclara. We do not share information received through TestFlight with third parties. Avoid including private contracts or other sensitive information in feedback or screenshots. Apple’s handling of this information is described in TestFlight & Privacy.

6. Support emails

If you email support@paclara.com, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use these details to respond to your request and resolve problems. Messages sent to this address are forwarded to our support inbox.

7. Website analytics

Paclara uses Cloudflare Web Analytics on the Paclara website to understand aggregate website usage, such as page views, visits, referring websites, countries, devices, browsers and operating systems.

Cloudflare Web Analytics is designed as a privacy-focused analytics service. It does not use cookies or localStorage to collect usage metrics and does not fingerprint individual visitors for analytics purposes.

We use these analytics only to understand how the Paclara website is being used and to improve the website and beta experience. We do not use this information for targeted advertising or to build profiles of individual visitors.

Cloudflare processes analytics data on our behalf in accordance with its own privacy and data-processing practices.

8. Sharing and retention

We do not sell your personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

Local Paclara content remains on your device until you remove it, Paclara permanently deletes it through an available deletion function, or the app’s data is deleted. Items placed in Recently Deleted remain recoverable for up to 72 hours before becoming eligible for permanent deletion.

If iCloud Sync is enabled, eligible Paclara data is also stored in your private iCloud database and changes may be synchronised across your participating devices. Deleted information may continue to exist temporarily in Apple-managed backups or other Apple systems according to Apple’s retention practices.

We retain support correspondence and separately retained beta feedback only for as long as needed to address requests, investigate issues, or meet applicable legal obligations. Information held within Apple’s TestFlight and iCloud services is also subject to Apple’s own retention rules.

9. Deletion and privacy requests

You can remove app data using Paclara’s available deletion controls or by deleting the app and its data from your device. Offloading the app may preserve its data. Exported Paclara backups and other device or cloud backups must be managed separately.

If iCloud Sync is enabled, deletions made in Paclara may also synchronise through your private iCloud database to other participating devices. Removing the app from one device does not necessarily remove copies stored on another device or in Apple-managed backups.

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold through support or beta testing, email support@paclara.com. We cannot directly access or delete collaboration data stored only on your device or in your private iCloud database.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Paclara or our data practices change. The revised policy will appear on this page with an updated date.
