Price a Deal

Take the guesswork out of pricing.

Your pricing history becomes your pricing model. Paclara turns your own completed deals into a suggested price and range, so you can send your next quote with more confidence.

Most influencer rate calculators start with generic CPMs or market averages. Paclara starts with you.

After you have recorded at least two completed collaborations, Price a Deal can suggest both a price and a realistic pricing range for new work using only your own historical data.

It considers what you actually charged, the deliverables involved, your follower count then and now, client size, usage and boosting terms, exclusivity and any boosting view commitment.

You are already doing the hard part: making work people want to watch and brands want to be part of. But once collaborations start arriving, you also become the person remembering deadlines, checking contracts, tracking restrictions, chasing payments and deciding what the next deal should be worth.

That invisible management work is still work. Paclara gives it somewhere to live, so your inbox, calendar and memory do not have to become your management agency.
