Price a Deal

Take the guesswork out of pricing.

Your pricing history becomes the foundation, not the answer. Paclara applies its own pricing model to your completed collaborations and the details of the new opportunity to suggest a price and realistic range for new work.

Unlike generic rate calculators built around broad market averages, Paclara uses your own creator data as the starting point and applies a consistent pricing model to help you work out what a new deal may be worth.

Once you have recorded at least two completed collaborations, Price a Deal can suggest both a price and a realistic pricing range for new work.
